# Find the Bats
## Hunt all the bats for witch so she can make her potions... Made for 3am Hackclub

##Description
###Its a small web game made by basic HTML CSS and Javascript (Because i am new into coding :3), in it you have to hunt bats in a dark spooky night, you have been provided with a flashlight.

##How To Play It?
###First you will be welcomed with a text, in which the witch is asking to hunt for bats so she can make potions tonight. You Have to click the **Start** button. After clicking on the start button you will be lead to a dark night in spooky forest, you will be given flashlight so you can see, the flashlight will move with your cursor, you have to find for bats, after you find a bat click on it, it will be moved to your inventory and your score will be increases by one, the goal is to find all 10 bats hiding in the forest.
